Psych Fest West is a Bay Area psych rock music community bringing together great bands, breweries, fans, record collectors and other curious humans.

We’re starting with intimate gatherings, listening parties and local live music events under Psych Fest West Presents, building toward the Psych Fest West music festival in Northern California.
