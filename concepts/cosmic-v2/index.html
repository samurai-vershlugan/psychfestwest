WHAT IS THIS?

Psych Fest West is a new Bay Area psychedelic rock music community bringing together fans, bands, breweries, record collectors and other curious humans.

We’re starting with intimate gatherings, listening parties and live events under Psych Fest West Presents, building toward an annual Psych Fest West music festival in Northern California.
