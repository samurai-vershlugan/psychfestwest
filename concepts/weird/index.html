Psych Fest West is a new Bay Area psychedelic rock music community bringing together fans, bands, breweries, record collectors and other curious humans.

We’re starting with gatherings, listening parties, live shows and other odd transmissions under Psych Fest West Presents, building toward an annual Northern California festival.
